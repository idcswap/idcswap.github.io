Privacy Policy

This is a personal application used only by its owner.

The application accesses Google Drive through the Google Drive API solely to create, read, update, and delete encrypted backup files created by rclone.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Authentication credentials are used only to authorize access to the owner's Google Drive account.

Revoking the application's Google Account access prevents any further access to Google Drive.
